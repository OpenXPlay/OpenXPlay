## Reporting a problem {#feedback}

When you report a compatibility problem, include the head unit model, its system version, the route you used and what happened. A report from [Check your head unit](/en/check) helps too. Before asking, have a look at [Troubleshooting](/en/troubleshooting).

## Sharing your head unit {#share-head-unit}

- Open [Check your head unit](/en/check) in the head unit’s browser, copy the report and post it with the head unit model and its system version. Reports help other drivers with the same head unit.
- On the Android relay, Settings → Car and screen can copy your head-unit screen data for the developers, so a later release can include your car; see [Choose your car](/en/daily-use#car-model).
